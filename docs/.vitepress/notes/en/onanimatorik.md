Happens just before the Animator updates its internal IK. It is the place to set IK goal positions and their weights. **Layer** is the index of the Animator layer the IK solver is called on.

On Animator Move happens before this event in the same frame ([On Animator Move](/en/reference/events/onanimatormove)).

Official docs: [MonoBehaviour.OnAnimatorIK (Unity)](https://docs.unity3d.com/2022.3/Documentation/ScriptReference/MonoBehaviour.OnAnimatorIK.html)
