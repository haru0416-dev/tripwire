Happens when the player changes graphics settings that affect one of the values VRCQualitySettings exposes: anti-aliasing, shadow resolution and distance, LOD bias, vSyncCount and a few more.

It may happen often, so keep the actions light. To read the values, search for VRCQualitySettings in **Call Udon API**.

Official docs: [Event Nodes (VRChat)](https://creators.vrchat.com/worlds/udon/graph/event-nodes/), [VRCQualitySettings (VRChat)](https://creators.vrchat.com/worlds/udon/vrc-graphics/vrc-quality-settings/)
