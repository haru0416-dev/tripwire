Happens when a player lets go of a VRC PhysBone they were holding. As with [On PhysBone Grabbed](/en/reference/events/onphysbonegrabbed), put the Tripwire Trigger on the PhysBone's object.

Freezing the bone in its grabbed shape (a pose) comes as [On PhysBone Posed](/en/reference/events/onphysboneposed).

Official docs: [PhysBones (VRChat)](https://creators.vrchat.com/common-components/physbones/)
