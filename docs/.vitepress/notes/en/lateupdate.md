Happens every frame while this trigger is enabled, after every Update has run. Unity recommends it for anything that follows things moved in Update, such as a follow camera.

To follow a player's bones, use Post Late Update, which runs after VRChat has calculated IK ([Post Late Update](/en/reference/events/postlateupdate)).

Official docs: [MonoBehaviour.LateUpdate (Unity)](https://docs.unity3d.com/2022.3/Documentation/ScriptReference/MonoBehaviour.LateUpdate.html)
