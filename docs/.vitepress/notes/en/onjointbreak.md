Happens when a joint on this object breaks because a force above its Break Force acts on it. **Break force** is the force on the joint when it broke.

After this event, Unity removes the joint from the object and deletes it. Use it for effects such as showing debris or playing a sound when something snaps off.

Official docs: [OnJointBreak (Unity)](https://docs.unity3d.com/2022.3/Documentation/ScriptReference/MonoBehaviour.OnJointBreak.html)
