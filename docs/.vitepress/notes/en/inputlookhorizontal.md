Happens when your left/right look input changes: the mouse on desktop, usually the right stick on gamepads. On VR controllers it comes from turning with the right stick without Comfort Turning.

The range and how often it fires are as in [Input Move Horizontal](/en/reference/events/inputmovehorizontal).

Official docs: [Input Events (VRChat)](https://creators.vrchat.com/worlds/udon/input-events/)
