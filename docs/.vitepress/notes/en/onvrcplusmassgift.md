Happens when anyone in the instance drops a batch of VRC+ gifts. **The giver** is the player who gave them, and **Number of gifts** is how many.

**Whose** on the card starts as **Me**, so the actions run only when you are the giver. To celebrate whoever gives, set it to **Anyone**.

Official docs: [Event Nodes (VRChat)](https://creators.vrchat.com/worlds/udon/graph/event-nodes/)
