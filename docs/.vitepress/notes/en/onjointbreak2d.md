Happens when a 2D joint on this object breaks: its reaction force went above its Break Force, or its reaction torque above its Break Torque. **The joint** is the Joint2D that broke.

What happens to the joint afterwards depends on its Break Action setting.

Official docs: [OnJointBreak2D (Unity)](https://docs.unity3d.com/2022.3/Documentation/ScriptReference/MonoBehaviour.OnJointBreak2D.html)
