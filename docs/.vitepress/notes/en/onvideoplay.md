Happens when playback starts: the next video in a queue starting, resuming from pause, or playing through player interaction. For starts from a stopped state only, use [On Video Start](/en/reference/events/onvideostart).

Together with [On Video Pause](/en/reference/events/onvideopause), it can dim the lights only while the video plays.

Official docs: [Event Nodes (VRChat)](https://creators.vrchat.com/worlds/udon/graph/event-nodes/)
