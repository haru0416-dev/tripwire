Happens on your screen when you release Use while holding the pickup. Paired with On Pickup Use Down, it can play a sound only while the button is held.

As with [On Pickup Use Down](/en/reference/events/onpickupusedown), on desktop it needs Auto Hold on the VRC Pickup.

Official docs: [Event Nodes (VRChat)](https://creators.vrchat.com/worlds/udon/graph/event-nodes/)
