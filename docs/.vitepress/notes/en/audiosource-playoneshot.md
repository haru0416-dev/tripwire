Plays the **Sound** once without stopping what the source is playing, so short sounds like button clicks can overlap. Volume and 3D settings come from the target AudioSource.

It stops neither what Play Audio is playing nor earlier one-shot sounds. It plays only on the screen where the action runs; to let everyone present hear it, set the card's Advanced setting to **Everyone's screen**.

Official docs: [AudioSource.PlayOneShot (Unity)](https://docs.unity3d.com/2022.3/Documentation/ScriptReference/AudioSource.PlayOneShot.html)
