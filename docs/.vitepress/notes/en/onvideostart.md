Happens when the video player starts playing from a stopped state. To also catch resuming from pause, use [On Video Play](/en/reference/events/onvideoplay).

It happens on each player's own screen when the video actually starts playing. VRChat's video players don't sync playback by themselves; [Theater](/en/recipes/theater) shows how to play for everyone.

Official docs: [Event Nodes (VRChat)](https://creators.vrchat.com/worlds/udon/graph/event-nodes/), [Udon Video Sync Player (VRChat)](https://creators.vrchat.com/worlds/examples/udon-example-scene/udon-video-sync-player/)
