Happens when a player on a mobile device enters the world, and whenever they turn the device. It happens on that player's screen only.

In **Screen data**, type says what happened (only OrientationChanged for now), orientation is Landscape or Portrait, and resolution is the screen resolution as a Vector2.

Use it to rearrange UI between portrait and landscape.

Official docs: [Event Nodes (VRChat)](https://creators.vrchat.com/worlds/udon/graph/event-nodes/)
