Happens when you press or release Drop: right click on desktop. In VR it is pressing the grip on Vive wands and some Windows Mixed Reality controllers, and releasing the grip on others.

handType in **Input details** tells which hand's button it was. Acting only on press works as in [Input Jump](/en/reference/events/inputjump).

Official docs: [Input Events (VRChat)](https://creators.vrchat.com/worlds/udon/input-events/)
