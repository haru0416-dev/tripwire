Happens when someone gets up from the station. VRChat's docs describe it as happening on your screen when you leave this object's station, so as with On Station Entered, **Whose** on **Me** is the safe choice.

It is often used to undo what sitting down changed. A station with Disable Station Exit on can't be left by the usual means, so provide another way to get players out ([On Station Entered](/en/reference/events/onstationentered)).

Official docs: [Event Nodes (VRChat)](https://creators.vrchat.com/worlds/udon/graph/event-nodes/), [VRC Station (VRChat)](https://creators.vrchat.com/worlds/components/vrc_station/)
