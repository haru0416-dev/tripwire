Happens on your screen as the answer to a Store.ListAvailableProducts call made there. **The products** holds every product (UdonProduct) used in this world.

Tripwire has no action that calls ListAvailableProducts. This event happens when another UdonSharp script makes the call with this trigger as the receiver.
