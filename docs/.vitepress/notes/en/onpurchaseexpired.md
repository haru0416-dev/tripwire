Happens when your client detects that a product owned by someone in the instance has expired.

In Build & Test, purchases expire after 60 seconds however many months you chose, which makes this event easy to try. In ClientSim, the UdonProducts Manager can expire products too. **Whose** on the card starts as **Me**.

Official docs: [Udon Documentation (VRChat)](https://creators.vrchat.com/economy/sdk/udon-documentation/), [Testing Udon Products (VRChat)](https://creators.vrchat.com/economy/sdk/testing/)
