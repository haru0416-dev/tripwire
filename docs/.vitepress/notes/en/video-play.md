Plays the loaded video; a paused video carries on from where it stopped. When playback starts, [On Video Play](/en/reference/events/onvideoplay) happens on triggers on the video player's object.

It plays only on the screen where the action runs: VRChat's video players don't sync by themselves. To control players like ProTV, use **Use another script** ([Video players](/en/guide/video-players)).

Official docs: [Video Players (VRChat)](https://creators.vrchat.com/worlds/udon/video-players/)
