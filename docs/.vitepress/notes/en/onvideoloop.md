With looping on, happens each time the video finishes and starts over. **Loop Video** turns looping on and off.

The synced video player prefabs in the SDK turn looping off to keep sync working. To make them loop, the official docs say to turn on Loop and remove the UdonBehaviour.

Official docs: [Event Nodes (VRChat)](https://creators.vrchat.com/worlds/udon/graph/event-nodes/), [Video Players (VRChat)](https://creators.vrchat.com/worlds/udon/video-players/)
