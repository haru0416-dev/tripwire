Happens when another collider stops touching this object's collider. **What left it** is a Collision.

It needs the same setup as On Collision Enter: both colliders with Is Trigger off, and a Rigidbody with Is Kinematic off on one of them ([On Collision Enter](/en/reference/events/oncollisionenter)).

Official docs: [OnCollisionExit (Unity)](https://docs.unity3d.com/2022.3/Documentation/ScriptReference/MonoBehaviour.OnCollisionExit.html)
