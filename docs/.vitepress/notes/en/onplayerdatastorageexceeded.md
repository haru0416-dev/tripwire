Happens when a player's PlayerData usage has gone over the storage limit. The limit is 100 KB per player for each world.

Data that would go over the limit can't be saved: VRChat logs an error and the data isn't saved. Save less data.

**Whose** on the card starts as **Me**. To find out earlier, use [the warning event](/en/reference/events/onplayerdatastoragewarning) for nearing the limit.

Official docs: [PlayerData (VRChat)](https://creators.vrchat.com/worlds/udon/persistence/player-data/), [Persistence (VRChat)](https://creators.vrchat.com/worlds/udon/persistence/)
