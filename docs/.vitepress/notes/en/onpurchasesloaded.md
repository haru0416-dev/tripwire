Happens when all of a player's purchases have been loaded: when you join an instance, and when another player joins later. It happens even for a player who owns nothing; **The products** is then empty.

Right after Start, purchases may not have arrived yet. When behaviour depends on who owns what, wait for this event before deciding. **Whose** on the card starts as **Me**.
