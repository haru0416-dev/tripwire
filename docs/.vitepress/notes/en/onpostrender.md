Happens only on a trigger attached to the same object as a Camera, just after that camera has rendered the scene.

A typical use is undoing a visual change made in On Pre Render ([On Pre Render](/en/reference/events/onprerender)). It works with cameras placed in the world, not the player's view camera.

Official docs: [MonoBehaviour.OnPostRender (Unity)](https://docs.unity3d.com/2022.3/Documentation/ScriptReference/MonoBehaviour.OnPostRender.html)
