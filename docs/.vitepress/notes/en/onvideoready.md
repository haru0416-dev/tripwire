Happens when the video player has loaded a new video. **Load Video URL** loads without playing; run **Play Video** in this event to start once loading is done.

To test in Play mode, use VRC Unity Video Player with a direct link to an mp4 or webm file. VRC AVPro Video Player doesn't play inside Unity, and links such as YouTube only work in VRChat.

Official docs: [Event Nodes (VRChat)](https://creators.vrchat.com/worlds/udon/graph/event-nodes/), [Video Players (VRChat)](https://creators.vrchat.com/worlds/udon/video-players/)
